{{- /* Feed body. Internal asset addresses are root-relative in the source and have to become absolute before they leave the site, or a reader resolves them against the feed reader's own host. `srcset` is here too since T66: a responsive body image puts several addresses in one attribute, and only its first one sits where a `src="/…"` rule could see it. The candidates after that are separated by `, ` — the second rule is scoped to `/assets/` rather than to any slash so it cannot fire inside prose that happens to contain a comma followed by a path. A reader that ignores still finds the fallback inside it, which is the narrow JPEG — the right file to send to a feed reader anyway. */ -}} {{- $baseURL := strings.TrimSuffix "/" .Site.BaseURL -}} {{- $content := .Content -}} {{- $content = replaceRE `(href|src|srcset)="/([^/])` (printf `${1}="%s/${2}` $baseURL) $content -}} {{- $content = replaceRE `, /assets/` (printf `, %s/assets/` $baseURL) $content -}} {{- $content | safeHTML -}}
